About

I am an aquatic biogeochemist studying how nutrients move through watersheds, sediments, and estuarine ecosystems. My research focuses on phosphorus cycling, sediment–water interactions, and harmful algal bloom dynamics in the Lake Pontchartrain Basin and coastal Louisiana.

Research

  • Legacy phosphorus in river sediments
  • Sediment disturbance and internal nutrient loading
  • Watershed-to-estuary nutrient transport
